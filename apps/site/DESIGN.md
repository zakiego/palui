# Palui site design

The public site at palui.zakiego.com: the landing page and the docs. This file holds the site's design contract and records design calls about it as they're made. The dashboard (apps/app) keeps its own.

## Contract

Written before building (anti-ui-slop step 1). The finish gate checks against it.

- Audience: engineers who land on palui.zakiego.com, mostly curious about what Zaki built, a few who want to read how it works.
- Primary task: understand in one screen what Palui is and what it does for you, then read the docs.
- Category default: an "AI agent" SaaS landing with a gradient hero, a feature grid, a chat screenshot and "Join the waitlist".
- Predictable opposite: a README rendered as a page.
- Neither. Palui's own idea is that every capability is a connector you can switch. The first screen is one sentence of positioning and one visual: Palui itself, a block mascot standing behind the three things it keeps for you (notes, an alarm clock, a toolbox). The picture has no labels: each thing acts out what it is for (a note gets written, the alarm rings, a hammer comes out of the toolbox). The visual shows what the visitor gets, not how the system is wired, and it has no switches in it.
- Content rules: one headline, one sentence, one call to action ("Read the docs"), one visual. No feature grid, no screenshots, no sign-up, no pricing, no invented numbers. The headline is one plain sentence with no period, "An agent friend you host yourself", and never a two-part tagline. No channel is named in the headline or the sentence under it (WhatsApp is not the main feature).
- Public copy: no secrets, no home paths, no internal hostnames or IPs, no machine names. Say "the Docker host" and "your tunnel".
- Look: dark and developer-plain, and its own look, not recall's. The hero visual is an isometric block mascot in flat grays, not a graph or a terminal window. IBM Plex Sans for prose and IBM Plex Mono for the headline and small labels. Mono: neutral black and grays with no hue (`#0a0a0a` page, `#111111` surface), and white as the only accent on dark (ink in light docs). No indigo, violet or purple, and no blue tint in the neutrals. Red, green and amber appear only as status colors in the docs. The landing is always dark; the docs open dark and can switch to light. English copy, kept simple: a few short readable lines, no Banjar text, no log wall. Every text color clears WCAG AA on dark (muted text 5.5:1). 0 gradients, 0 emoji, radius only from the `--radius` steps.
- Mark: an original geometric drawing inspired by Si Palui (a kopiah over two dots and a smile, on a plain tile: white with an ink face on dark, ink with a white face on light). Not the comic's art. Paths live in `components/palui-mark.tsx` and `lib/og.tsx`. The landing mascot (`components/landing/mascot-scene.tsx`, drawn with the kit in `iso.tsx`) is the same character in 3D: the tile extruded into a head with the mark's own eyes and smile on its front, and the kopiah as a real box on top. It is real SVG, every solid is three flat grays (top, left, right), and there are no gradients. The home social card draws the same scene. The favicons (`app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png` and the app's `public/app/favicon.svg`) are the mascot's head with its kopiah on an ink tile, built by `scripts/favicon.mjs`. Rerun it after changing the head.
- States and sizes: static page, so no loading or error states. Works at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 wide. On a phone the copy and the whole mascot sit in the first screen, copy first.
- Motion: the mascot acts (`components/landing/mascot-hero.tsx`, clock in `lib/mascot.ts`, motion in `landing.css`). On load the copy settles in, then Palui and its slab rise as one piece (260 ms), then the three things land one after another (280 ms each, starting at 300, 390 and 470 ms, from 14, 12 and 10 px up). After 1.4 s the loop starts: one thing has the stage for 2.4 s, everything rests 1.2 s, then the next thing goes, so a round takes 10.8 s. While a thing acts, Palui's eyes ease over to it and its head tips that way (4 degrees toward the notes or the toolbox, a 2.5 px nod at the clock, 280 ms). Notes: a fresh sheet drops 18 px onto the stack (300 ms), then its two lines are written left to right (280 and 240 ms), and afterwards it sinks 4 px into the stack and fades (240 ms). Clock: it rattles on its feet in two bursts of five swings (up to 6 degrees, 70 ms a swing, 1.4 s in all) while three ring marks flash above it. Toolbox: the lid slides back (260 ms), a hammer rises out of the box (300 ms) and taps twice (720 ms), then the hammer drops (200 ms) and the lid closes (240 ms). Palui blinks every 4.6 s, and on a device with a mouse its eyes follow the pointer between acts. The loop and the blink stop offscreen and in a hidden tab. Reduced motion gets the still picture with no loop and no blink. There is no pause control (see Decisions, 2026-11-11), which leaves WCAG 2.2.2 unmet for anyone without reduced motion set. UI motion is ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`) and 300 ms or less; the two swings (rattle, tap) use easeInOutSine. Transform and opacity only, plus a `clip-path` wipe for the written lines; hover motion is gated to fine pointers.

## Exemptions

- The landing's CTA (`.lp-cta`) is a styled link, not shadcn `Button`. Button's `transition-all` and `translate-y-px` press would fight the landing's own press and focus styles.
- The mascot is hand-written SVG. No shadcn component draws a character, and it is a picture (`role="img"` with a text alternative), so nothing in it can be focused. Docs pages use shadcn and Fumadocs components as usual.

## Decisions

- 2026-11-10, landing redesign. The first landing copied recall.zakiego.com (Fumadocs header, Geist on light zinc, a rounded node graph), and Zaki asked for a redesign: "You literally copy recall.zakiego.com, please redesign". Explore round 1 tried Panel, Komik and Sungai. Round 2 tried Panel, Terminal and Skema, all with Banjar copy. Zaki kept Terminal and asked for it simpler, in English and dark: "I want keep english, lets like before. Please make like a dev, but make it simple bro. Keep it dark". What shipped: the Terminal look (Plex, graphite, one indigo accent, a `palui-runtime ~ logs -f` window) cut down to one message's journey in seven short lines, with a short time, an icon and connector tag, and a plain-English message instead of log levels and key=value tails. The English headline, sentence and call to action from before came back. The docs moved to the same fonts and accent and open dark, so the two halves of the site read as one product. The old hero variants (Graph, Replay, Board), the picker and the prototypes are deleted.
- 2026-11-10, mono and a plain headline. Zaki rejected the indigo accent and the tagline: "I dont like the color, its ai slop" and "I dont like the copywriting 'personal agent, self hosted.' Its ai slop". Explore round 3 tried three palettes on the same terminal: Mono, Amber and Green. Zaki kept Mono ("I love mono"). For the headline he asked for no period, no "I" or "my", said "Whatsapp is not main feature" and gave the keyword "agent-friend", then left the pick open, so "An agent friend you host yourself" is the agent's pick. The sentence under it no longer names a channel. The docs dropped the indigo ramp and the blue tint in zinc, so primary is ink in light and near white in dark. The OG image, the icons and the theme color moved to the same black.
- 2026-11-10, graph instead of terminal. Zaki asked: "Change terminal to graph". The hero is now a hub-and-bus graph that replays the same story as the terminal did, with no channel named ("a message comes in", the first node is "You, any chat"). It keeps Mono, Plex Mono and square corners so it does not read as recall's rounded node graph. Nodes light by border and icon, wires by color, and a paused connector also changes its label and border style, so no state depends on color alone.
- 2026-11-11, mascot instead of graph. Zaki rejected the graph hero: "I still dont know what the graph means what benefit for user to understand easily", and asked for an SVG 3D mascot. Explore round 1 tried Block (the mark's tile extruded into an isometric figure in flat grays, behind three switches you flip), Clay (a soft lit bust with gradients and a typed speech bubble) and Layers (the mark cut into sheets stacked in CSS 3D, turning to the pointer). Zaki kept Block's figure and rejected its switches: "I love block, but i dont like the switch". Round 2 kept the block figure and dropped every switch from the visual: Solo (alone, one hand up), Says (a typed speech bubble) and Keeps (a stack of notes, an alarm clock and a toolbox, with labels). Zaki kept Keeps and asked "Is the mascot have movement or alive?", so it blinks, follows the pointer and looks at each of its three things in turn. The graph, its story and the switch drawings are deleted.
- 2026-11-11, no labels, no pause, more movement. Zaki sent a screenshot of the three label chips and asked: "remove this and pausa on animation, and make the animation move or do something". The Remembers, Reminds and Uses tools chips and the Pause button are gone. Before, Palui only looked at a thing while it lifted 7 px, with 4.4 s of stillness between looks. Now each thing acts out its meaning in turn (a note is written, the alarm rings, a hammer comes out of the toolbox and taps) and the rests are 1.2 s, so the picture explains itself without labels. The acts are the agent's pick, not Zaki's.
