# Palui site design

The public site at palui.zakiego.com: the landing page and the docs. This file holds the site's design contract and records design calls about it as they're made. The dashboard (apps/app) keeps its own.

## Contract

Written before building (anti-ui-slop step 1). The finish gate checks against it.

- Audience: engineers who land on palui.zakiego.com, mostly curious about what Zaki built, a few who want to read how it works.
- Primary task: understand in one screen what Palui is and what it does for you, see it do those things further down, then read the docs.
- Category default: an "AI agent" SaaS landing with a purple gradient hero, a feature grid of icons and paragraphs, a chat screenshot and "Join the waitlist".
- Predictable opposite: a README rendered as a page.
- Neither. The page is quiet in color and busy in content: it shows the product working instead of describing it. The hero is Palui itself, a block mascot behind the three things it keeps for you (notes, an alarm clock, a toolbox). An ask types into a box above it, the matching thing acts, and Palui answers, with the tools it can reach wired around it. Below, every box is a working demo of something only this product does: a tool that waits for Approve or Deny, a reminder with Done and its three snoozes, the five connector kinds on switches, a scheduled job, the two real install commands. No box is an icon with a paragraph.
- Content rules: the page is a hero (headline, one sentence, two calls to action, the stage), seven boxes under one heading, and a closing line with two calls to action, split by full-width hairlines. Every box is a title of five words or fewer, one line of about ten words, and a demo. No screenshots, no sign-up, no pricing, no testimonials, no invented numbers or customers, and no tag or pill above the headline. The headline is one plain sentence with no period, "An agent friend you host yourself", set in the text color (never a gradient), and never a two-part tagline. No channel is named anywhere on the page (WhatsApp is not the main feature), and no third-party service is named or drawn: demos say Calendar, Memory, Web. Every command shown is copied from the getting started page.
- Public copy: no secrets, no home paths, no internal hostnames or IPs, no machine names. Say "the Docker host" and "your tunnel".
- Look: dark and developer-plain, after the Linear reference in the owner's taste log, in the dashboard's night tokens (`apps/app/src/styles/app.css`), so the landing and the dashboard read as one product. Neutrals do all the work: `#08090a` page, `#0f1011` surface, `#141516` raised, `#23252a` lines, text `#f7f8f8`, muted `#8a8f98`, and white for anything you press (`#e5e5e6` at rest). One indigo, `#5e6ad2`, as in the dashboard: the focus ring, text selection and a switch that's on, never a fill bigger than that. No colored glow, no gradient text, no violet or purple. A gradient only fades a hairline or lights the top of the hero's frame, in white at a low alpha. Hairlines over boxes: the seven boxes are cells of one frame split by 1px rules, and only real objects (a chat, a task, a note, a terminal) get a filled panel. The header is a blurred glass bar. Status in the demos is drawn the dashboard's way, an icon with a word next to it: a gray ring while a step waits, a turning ring while it works, a green check (`#4cb782`) once it's done, and an indigo switch when a connector is on. Inter at 510 for headlines and 590 for titles (never 700), with `cv01` and `ss03`, Geist Mono for values a machine wrote (times, schedules, tool classes, commands), and a two-tone heading in place of a subtitle. Both load only on the landing (`lib/landing-fonts.ts`). Buttons are compact pills (32px tall, 13px label), 44px only on the closing pair. The landing is always dark; the docs open dark, can switch to light, and wear the same tokens and faces (see `app/docs/DESIGN.md`). English copy, kept simple, no Banjar text. Every text color clears WCAG AA on dark (muted text 6.1:1). 0 emoji, and panels take the dashboard's radius steps (6, 8, 12px); buttons, the nav link and switches are pills.
- Mark: an original geometric drawing inspired by Si Palui (a kopiah over two dots and a smile, on a plain tile: white with an ink face on dark, ink with a white face on light). Not the comic's art. Paths live in `components/palui-mark.tsx` and `lib/og.tsx`. The landing mascot (`components/landing/mascot-scene.tsx`, drawn with the kit in `iso.tsx`) is the same character in 3D: the tile extruded into a head with the mark's own eyes and smile on its front, and the kopiah as a real box on top. It is real SVG, every solid is three flat grays (top, left, right), and there are no gradients. The home social card draws the same scene. The favicons (`app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png` and the app's `public/app/favicon.svg`) are the mascot's head with its kopiah on an ink tile, built by `scripts/brand-icons.mjs`, which also writes the `/brand` downloads to `public/brand` (seven profile pictures, each dark and light, as 1024 px PNG and SVG, and the mark on dark and on light). Rerun it after changing the head or the mark.
- States and sizes: static page, so no loading or error states. Works at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440 wide. On a phone the copy and the whole mascot sit in the first screen, copy first, and the tools around Palui only show from 940px, where there is room on both sides. From 1024px the headline sits on the left with the sentence and the buttons low on the right. The boxes are one column on a phone, six columns from 640px and twelve from 1024px.
- Motion: the headline's entrance and the demos, and nothing else, both behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`. The headline comes into focus once on load, and the demos loop. Nothing reveals on scroll, nothing drifts in the background, and nothing moves on hover (hover brightens, a press scales to 0.97). Headline: each word goes from opacity 0, 10px of blur and 20% down to clear over 1000 ms (`cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1)`), 40 ms after the word before it. Every looping demo runs on one hook (`components/landing/use-player.ts`): it starts from its first step, never the finished picture, holds while it's offscreen or the tab is hidden, and with reduced motion shows one finished step. The hero demo opens with its own entrance, which is part of the demo: Palui and its slab rise as one piece (260 ms, after 120 ms), then the three things land one after another (280 ms each, at 300, 390 and 470 ms, from 14, 12 and 10 px up). The hero loop is in `lib/mascot.ts`: an ask is typed at 55 ms a character with no caret, 350 ms later the matching thing has the stage for 2.4 s while Palui's eyes and head turn to it, its tool tile brightens and a light runs down that tool's line (1.2 s, linear), the answer shows for 1.9 s, and the box clears, so a round takes about 20 s. The acts are unchanged: a sheet drops and its two lines are written, the clock rattles in two bursts with three ring marks, the lid slides back and a hammer taps twice. Palui blinks every 4.6 s and its eyes follow a mouse between acts. In the boxes, messages and steps rise in (240 ms), a working step spins (700 ms a turn), a check pops in (200 ms, from 0.9), a pressed button scales to 0.97, switches slide (240 ms), and the bell swings. There is no pause control (see Decisions, 2026-11-11), which leaves WCAG 2.2.2 unmet for anyone without reduced motion set. UI motion is ease-out (`cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)`) and 300 ms or less; swings use easeInOutSine. Transform and opacity only, except the headline's one-time blur, the `clip-path` wipe for the written lines and the dash offset of the one line whose tool is in use.

## Exemptions

- The landing's CTA (`.lp-cta`) is a styled link, not shadcn `Button`. Button's `transition-all` and `translate-y-px` press would fight the landing's own press and focus styles.
- The demos in the boxes (`components/landing/demos.tsx`) are pictures of product UI, each `role="img"` with a text alternative, so their buttons, switches and message boxes are drawn spans, not shadcn components, and nothing in them can be focused or pressed.
- Buttons and the nav link on the landing are pills (radius 9999), outside the `--radius` steps, after the Linear reference.
- The mascot is hand-written SVG. No shadcn component draws a character, and it is a picture (`role="img"` with a text alternative), so nothing in it can be focused. Docs pages use shadcn and Fumadocs components as usual.

## Decisions

- 2026-11-10, landing redesign. The first landing copied recall.zakiego.com (Fumadocs header, Geist on light zinc, a rounded node graph), and Zaki asked for a redesign: "You literally copy recall.zakiego.com, please redesign". Explore round 1 tried Panel, Komik and Sungai. Round 2 tried Panel, Terminal and Skema, all with Banjar copy. Zaki kept Terminal and asked for it simpler, in English and dark: "I want keep english, lets like before. Please make like a dev, but make it simple bro. Keep it dark". What shipped: the Terminal look (Plex, graphite, one indigo accent, a `palui-runtime ~ logs -f` window) cut down to one message's journey in seven short lines, with a short time, an icon and connector tag, and a plain-English message instead of log levels and key=value tails. The English headline, sentence and call to action from before came back. The docs moved to the same fonts and accent and open dark, so the two halves of the site read as one product. The old hero variants (Graph, Replay, Board), the picker and the prototypes are deleted.
- 2026-11-10, mono and a plain headline. Zaki rejected the indigo accent and the tagline: "I dont like the color, its ai slop" and "I dont like the copywriting 'personal agent, self hosted.' Its ai slop". Explore round 3 tried three palettes on the same terminal: Mono, Amber and Green. Zaki kept Mono ("I love mono"). For the headline he asked for no period, no "I" or "my", said "Whatsapp is not main feature" and gave the keyword "agent-friend", then left the pick open, so "An agent friend you host yourself" is the agent's pick. The sentence under it no longer names a channel. The docs dropped the indigo ramp and the blue tint in zinc, so primary is ink in light and near white in dark. The OG image, the icons and the theme color moved to the same black.
- 2026-11-10, graph instead of terminal. Zaki asked: "Change terminal to graph". The hero is now a hub-and-bus graph that replays the same story as the terminal did, with no channel named ("a message comes in", the first node is "You, any chat"). It keeps Mono, Plex Mono and square corners so it does not read as recall's rounded node graph. Nodes light by border and icon, wires by color, and a paused connector also changes its label and border style, so no state depends on color alone.
- 2026-11-11, mascot instead of graph. Zaki rejected the graph hero: "I still dont know what the graph means what benefit for user to understand easily", and asked for an SVG 3D mascot. Explore round 1 tried Block (the mark's tile extruded into an isometric figure in flat grays, behind three switches you flip), Clay (a soft lit bust with gradients and a typed speech bubble) and Layers (the mark cut into sheets stacked in CSS 3D, turning to the pointer). Zaki kept Block's figure and rejected its switches: "I love block, but i dont like the switch". Round 2 kept the block figure and dropped every switch from the visual: Solo (alone, one hand up), Says (a typed speech bubble) and Keeps (a stack of notes, an alarm clock and a toolbox, with labels). Zaki kept Keeps and asked "Is the mascot have movement or alive?", so it blinks, follows the pointer and looks at each of its three things in turn. The graph, its story and the switch drawings are deleted.
- 2026-11-11, no labels, no pause, more movement. Zaki sent a screenshot of the three label chips and asked: "remove this and pausa on animation, and make the animation move or do something". The Remembers, Reminds and Uses tools chips and the Pause button are gone. Before, Palui only looked at a thing while it lifted 7 px, with 4.4 s of stillness between looks. Now each thing acts out its meaning in turn (a note is written, the alarm rings, a hammer comes out of the toolbox and taps) and the rests are 1.2 s, so the picture explains itself without labels. The acts are the agent's pick, not Zaki's.
- 2026-11-11, brand page. Zaki asked for a page like vorneo.id/brand where he can download a Palui avatar for a WhatsApp or Telegram profile picture. `/brand` uses the landing's chrome and tokens and is linked from the landing footer, not the header, so the header keeps its one link. It leads with the profile pictures, each a full-bleed square because both apps crop to a circle, with how to set it on WhatsApp and through @BotFather. Then the mark, the colors, the type and the naming rules. Zaki then asked for dark and light versions, a straight-on 3D head and the full body, plus anything else that works as a profile picture. Then "Palui wave hand", so there are seven designs, each dark and light: the 3D head from the corner, the same head straight on (one-point perspective from above, so the tops show), Palui waving (the front arm out past the head with two wave marks), a portrait cropped at the shoulders, the full body on its shadow, the flat mark as an app icon, and the flat face. The 3D light versions sit on `#c8c8c8`, not white, because the head's white top and `#e6e6e6` front vanish on anything lighter. The portrait, the app icon and the order are the agent's pick.
- 2026-11-13, the dreamy agent landing. Zaki asked to "redesign the landing page to be more ai agent landing page". The first pass kept the quiet page and put three hero variants behind a picker: Thread (a chat with Palui, your seat), Run (one task step by step with its approval, the agent's seat) and Stage (the mascot under a box that asks type into, the character). Before picking, Zaki redirected: "The landing must be lot of things, like gradient, animation, blur, boxes, line, and else. Make modern feels agentic fantasy dream". That reverses the 2026-11-10 mono call and the one-visual rule for this page, so the contract above is rewritten. What shipped keeps all three: Stage is the hero, and Thread and Run became two of the seven boxes. The aurora's colors (mint, lime, amber, sky, and no purple because of the 2026-11-10 "ai slop" call on indigo), the tool tiles, the things in the drifting rows, the seven boxes and their one-line copy, and the closing "Bring Palui home" are the agent's picks, not Zaki's. The sentence under the headline changed to say what Palui does, and a second call to action, "Get started", joined "Read the docs". The social card still draws the mascot on plain black.
- 2026-11-13, no tag above the headline, and a warm aurora. Zaki on the first render: "Good. Give another color for graident", and about the "Self-hosted AI agent" pill above the headline, "remove this, i dont like this tag". The pill is gone and nothing took its place. The aurora moved from mint, lime, amber and sky to rose, peach, gold and orange, which is the agent's pick. Checks, working rings and switches in the demos turned white, because a rose check reads as an error.
- 2026-11-13, back to neutrals. Zaki on the warm aurora: "bad, still ai slop. please dont gradient agent friend" and "use our taste.md". The aurora, the gradient headline phrase, the colored glows and tints, the glass cards, the stars and the grid, the drifting rows of asks, the scroll reveal and every ambient loop are gone. The page now follows the Linear reference in the taste log: neutrals only, hairlines instead of filled cards, a left-aligned hero over a framed stage, compact pill buttons, the headline's word-by-word focus as the only entrance, and looping demos as the only other motion. What stays from the earlier rounds is the content: the acting mascot hero and the seven demo boxes. Where Palui's own look and the reference differ, Palui's wins: IBM Plex instead of Inter, hue-free grays instead of blue-tinted ones, no indigo accent at all, and no text dimmer than 5.7:1. Those four calls and cutting the rows of asks are the agent's picks, not Zaki's.
- 2026-11-13, dashboard tokens on the landing (the agent's pick, the owner wasn't watching the run). The owner asked for the landing in Linear's look, consistent with the dashboard redesign (PR #93): its palette, Inter and radius steps, with any faux UI matching the new dashboard. That's a clear brief (a named reference and a sibling to match), and the page's structure had just been settled in the round above, so the run kept every section and changed the skin. Before #94 landed, an Explore built three hero visuals on the older one-visual page: 1 Mascot on a hairline stage (axis: closest to these Decisions), 2 Chat, a looping web chat where Palui sets a reminder and finds a note (axis: the owner's seat), 3 Dashboard, the dashboard's Home drawn at desktop size (axis: Linear's landing template). Mascot was the pick, since Chat is the contract's category default and Dashboard shows wiring, not benefit, at 6 px text on a phone. #94's page already keeps the mascot as the hero and puts the chat and the product states in the boxes, so that pick carries over unchanged. What changed: Plex to Inter and Geist Mono, `#0a0a0a` to the dashboard's `#08090a` ramp, radius 10 and 14 to 8 and 12, white focus to the indigo ring, and the demos' states from brightness only to the dashboard's green check and indigo switch. This reverses "status is never a hue" from the round above, because the owner's taste log asks for status as a colored icon plus text and the dashboard draws it that way. The social card moved to the same look (Inter, `#08090a`, the mascot on its stage, no terminal window), docs cards included.
