---
title: Approvals
description: Risky tool calls wait for your yes. Pending approvals are saved, survive restarts, and turn into a denial after 10 minutes.
---

Some tool calls need your yes before they run. Which ones depends on the [tool class](/docs/concepts/tool-classes):

- `destructive` tools always need approval.
- `write` tools need approval in [tainted turns](/docs/concepts/taint-rule) and in [scheduled jobs](/docs/concepts/jobs).

## How it works

<ApprovalDiagram>
  The model calls a tool that needs approval. The turn ends instead of waiting, and the request is saved as a pending approval. You get a prompt on your channel. Your answer, ya or batal or a button press, resumes the turn: approved tools run and the model carries on. If you don't answer within 10 minutes, the turn resumes with a denial.
</ApprovalDiagram>

1. The model asks to call a tool that needs approval.
2. The turn ends right there. The request and the conversation so far are saved as a pending approval, and the chat's queue is freed, so the chat isn't blocked while you think.
3. You get a prompt that names the tool and what it will do.
4. Your answer resumes the turn with a new model call that carries your decision. If you approved, the tool runs. If you denied, the model is told and can reply accordingly.

Because the pending approval lives in the database, it survives a restart or a deploy. You can answer after Palui comes back up.

## Answering

| Where | Approve | Deny |
|---|---|---|
| Telegram | The approve button | The deny button |
| WhatsApp | Reply `ya` (yes) | Reply `batal` (cancel) |
| Dashboard | Approve on the Chats page | Deny on the Chats page |

The answer is matched by the router before the model sees anything. A "ya" that answers an approval isn't sent to the model as a message.

## Expiry

A pending approval expires after 10 minutes. The turn then resumes with a denial, so the model can close the loop ("OK, I didn't delete it") instead of leaving the conversation hanging.

## Why not just a confirmation in the prompt

Telling the model "ask before deleting" is a label in the prompt, and a prompt injection can talk the model out of it. Approvals are enforced by the runtime: the tool call physically doesn't run until you answer.
