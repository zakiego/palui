---
title: Agent bot (Telegram)
description: Give an agent its own Telegram bot, so people can add it to a group and @mention it by name.
---

Kind: `channel`, several allowed. It's the [Telegram](/docs/connectors/telegram) connector under another name: one bot per agent. Every chat that comes in on this bot belongs to its agent. See [Agents](/docs/concepts/agents).

## Connect

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the bot

Message @BotFather, send `/newbot` and copy the token. Leave privacy mode on: in groups the bot then sees only commands, mentions and replies to it.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add it to Palui

On the Connectors page, add "Agent bot (Telegram)" and paste the token. Test shows the bot's `@username`.

</Step>
<Step>

### Give it to an agent

Open the agent on the Agents page and pick the bot under "Own Telegram bot".

</Step>
</Steps>

## How it behaves

- You're the owner on every bot. Claiming the main Telegram bot is enough.
- A chat with this bot is its own chat, separate from the same DM or group on the main bot, and its agent can't be changed.
- Replies go out through the bot the message came in on.
- Other people are answered only in chats you allowed on the Chats page.
