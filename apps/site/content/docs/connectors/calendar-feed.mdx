title: Calendar feed
description: Read any calendar from its secret iCal link, with no OAuth app to set up.
---

Kind: `tools`. A calendar feed reads a calendar from its iCalendar link (RFC 5545), the format every calendar service can publish. It works with Google Calendar, iCloud, Outlook, Fastmail, Nextcloud and anything else that gives you an `.ics` address. It's read only, and you can add as many as you like: one per calendar.

It's the easiest way to give [Proactive](/docs/connectors/proactive) a calendar: mention `@<connector id>__get_events` in the prompt.

## Connect

1. Copy the calendar's private iCal address:
   - **Google Calendar**: Settings, pick the calendar, then **Secret address in iCal format**.
   - **iCloud**: share the calendar as a public calendar and copy the `webcal://` link.
   - **Outlook**: Settings, Calendar, Shared calendars, **Publish a calendar**, then the ICS link.
   - **Fastmail, Nextcloud**: the calendar's subscription link.
2. On the Connectors page, add **Calendar feed** and paste the address.
3. Optionally add your email addresses, separated by commas, so invites you declined show as declined.
4. Press Test. It reads the next 7 days and says how many events it found.

## Settings

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| iCal address | The feed link. It's a secret: anyone with it can read the calendar, so it's encrypted at rest and never shown again. `webcal://` links are read over https. |
| Your email addresses | Optional. An event where one of these addresses declined is marked `declined`. |

## Tools

- `get_events` (read) lists the events in a time range, sorted by start. It takes `from` and `to` (an ISO date-time or a date) or `days` (1 to 31, default 2 from now). Recurring events are expanded, each occurrence with its own stable id, and moved or skipped occurrences are respected. Each event has a title, start and end in your time zone (a date for all-day events), location, a video call link when there is one, and `declined` when you declined it. Cancelled events are left out.

The feed is fetched at most every 5 minutes. Calendar services refresh their iCal links on their own schedule, from minutes (Fastmail, Nextcloud) to a few hours (Google), so a brand-new event may take a while to show up.

## Safety

<Callout type="warn" title="Event text is outside text">
  Anyone can put text in your calendar by sending an invite. Results from this connector taint the turn, so with the agent's "Ask before writes after reading outside content" switch on, any write after them asks you first.
</Callout>

- Errors never show the secret part of the link, only its host.
- Time zones are read from the feed. IANA names (what Google, iCloud and Fastmail write) and the common Windows names Outlook writes are understood; anything else falls back to your time zone.

## Pause and remove

Pausing hides the tool. Removing deletes the link from Palui; the calendar isn't touched. To cut off access completely, reset the secret address in your calendar's settings.
