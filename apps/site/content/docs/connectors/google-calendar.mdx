---
title: Google Calendar
description: Give Palui read-only access to your Google Calendar so it can answer questions about your schedule and include events in the briefing.
---

Kind: `skill`. Google Calendar gives the model read-only access to your calendar. It can answer "what's on tomorrow?" and add today's events to the [daily briefing](/docs/connectors/jobs#daily-briefing). It can't create, change or delete events.

<Callout type="info" title="Status">
  Google Calendar is in v1 as a read-only connector. It ships in a later v1 milestone, after the core connectors, so the setup below may still change.
</Callout>

## Connect

1. On the Connectors page, add Google Calendar.
2. Follow the sign-in step (planned: a Google OAuth sign-in that grants read access to your calendar).
3. Press Test.

## Settings

Your Google account connection. The OAuth tokens are secrets: encrypted at rest and never shown.

## Tools

Read-only calendar tools. The exact tool list, with each tool's switch, is shown on the connector page in the dashboard.

## Pause and remove

Pausing applies from the next turn, and the daily briefing runs without calendar events. Removing deletes the stored OAuth tokens.
