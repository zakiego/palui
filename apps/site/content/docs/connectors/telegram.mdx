Kind: `channel`. Chat with Palui from a Telegram bot you create. It's the most reliable channel, and the one with buttons for reminders, approvals and `/connectors`.

## Connect

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create a bot

In Telegram, message @BotFather, send `/newbot` and follow the prompts. Copy the token it gives you (it looks like `<bot-id>:<your-bot-token>`).

</Step>
<Step>

### Paste the token and test

On the Connectors page, add Telegram and paste the token. Test calls Telegram's `getMe` and shows the bot's `@bot_username` when the token works.

</Step>
<Step>

### Claim owner

The wizard shows a `t.me/<bot>?start=<nonce>` link and a QR code. Open it on your phone and press Start. The nonce is single use and expires after 10 minutes. Only a `/start` with that nonce claims owner, and the wizard waits with a live status line until it arrives.

</Step>
</Steps>

From then on, messages from your Telegram account reach Palui. Everyone else is dropped silently.

## Settings

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Bot token | Secret. Write-only, shown masked. Replace it if you regenerate the token in @BotFather. |

## How it behaves

- Long polling. No public webhook URL needed.
- Shows the typing action while a turn runs.
- Replies are sent as HTML and split at 4096 characters.
- Media in: photos (read by the `vision` model role), voice messages (transcribed by [Voice-in](/docs/connectors/voice-in)) and small documents (text extracted).
- Inline keyboards for reminder actions (Done, Snooze 10m, 1h, tomorrow 09.00), approvals and `/connectors`.
- DMs only in v1. Group chats are planned.

## Tools

None. Telegram is a channel: it carries messages, it doesn't give the model tools.

## Pause and remove

- Pause: polling stops. Replies and reminders waiting in the outbox stay queued without using up attempts and send when you resume. The owner claim and token are kept.
- Remove: deletes the token. Waiting rows go to your fallback channel if one is on, otherwise they're cancelled.
- `/connectors off telegram` sent from Telegram only works when another channel or the dashboard is reachable. See [Chat commands](/docs/chat-commands).

<Callout type="info" title="One consumer per token">
  Telegram allows one long-polling consumer per bot token. If you run a second Palui (for development), give it its own bot.
</Callout>
